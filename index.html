HOW TO PLAY & RULES

🪙 Toss & Preference Choice

Toss winner bats first and picks EVEN or ODD balls as their preference. The bowling team gets the opposite.

★ On Preferred Balls (ATTACK!)

• Run Scored: +1 BONUS BALL added to your quota! (Innings extended!)
• Dot Ball: -1 Ball Penalty (costs 2 balls total).
• Wicket: -1 Ball Penalty + 1 Wicket lost.

◇ On Non-Preferred Balls (DEFEND!)

• Dot Ball: SAFE (0 Penalty) — Safely preserve your balls!
• Run Scored: -1 Ball Penalty (Risky to hit!).
• Wicket: -1 Ball Penalty + 1 Wicket lost.
• ⚠️ 3-Strike Penalty: Scoring runs on 3 consecutive non-preferred balls triggers an immediate 1 WICKET PENALTY!

⚡ Controls & Shortcuts

• Click/Tap SWING BAT or press SPACEBAR to hit.
• Click/Tap DEFEND or press D for a safe dot ball.
• Press P or tap ⏸️ to pause the match.

🏆 Match Result

Highest score after both innings wins! Ties occur if runs are strictly identical.
